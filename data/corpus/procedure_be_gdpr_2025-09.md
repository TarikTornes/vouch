# Data protection in payroll

- Payroll files contain personal data: only share them through the secure client portal, never by email attachment.
- Keep payslip records for five years after the end of employment, then delete them.
- Report any data incident to the privacy team on the same day.
- Do not paste client payroll data into public AI tools.
