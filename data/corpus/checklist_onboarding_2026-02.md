# Onboarding checklist for new payroll consultants

1. Request access to the payroll engine and the client file share.
2. Read the Belgian payroll policy.
3. Shadow a senior consultant for one full payroll run.
4. Meet each client's HR contact within your first month.
5. Complete the GDPR training module.
