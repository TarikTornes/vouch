# Belgian Payroll Policy — version May 2026

This policy applies to all Belgian clients unless a signed client agreement states otherwise.
It replaces the payroll procedure of March 2023.

## Overtime
Saturday hours count as overtime.
The overtime surcharge for Saturday hours is 50%.
The overtime surcharge for Sunday hours is 100%.
Public holiday arrangements follow the sector (joint committee) agreement — see client file.

## Meal vouchers
The employer part of the meal voucher is €8 per working day.
